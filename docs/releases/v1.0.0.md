**Live at <https://jeisey.github.io/jeisey-tiers/>**, refreshing itself daily at 07:17 America/New_York.

A fantasy-football draft board built the other way round from most: the value model never sees a market price, and the market comparison is kept strictly downstream of it. That separation is the product, not an implementation detail — an intrinsic model that has learned ADP cannot tell you the market is wrong about anyone.

## What shipped

- **An intrinsic value model.** `intrinsic-cb-hurdle-v1`, an availability × per-game-performance hurdle over LightGBM quantile boosters, trained on 2014–2025 and promoted through a sealed single-use holdout it was evaluated against exactly once. Its feature set is firewalled: no ADP, no ECR, no expert or consensus rank, no sportsbook line, no output of the arbitrage board. A test walks the import graph and fails on any path from an intrinsic module to market data.
- **Probabilistic value above replacement.** 10,000 Monte Carlo draws per player per league, each one allocating starters and FLEX and deriving *that draw's* replacement level, so VORP carries league scarcity rather than being points minus a constant.
- **League-aware tiers.** Contiguous groups discovered from simulated value by dynamic-programming segmentation — the count is found, not fixed per position.
- **A deterministic arbitrage board.** `a0_rank_gap_v1`: fair rank against MyFantasyLeague ADP, scored as a within-preset percentile. Transparent by design and labelled as a baseline everywhere.
- **Market trends from retained snapshots.** Point-in-time ADP captured daily into a private append-only store, because MFL's historical export is a season aggregate recomputed at request time — a price not captured today can never be reconstructed.
- **Current injury and roster status** from Sleeper, published as a separate artifact that annotates a row and can never move one. Every surface that shows a designation says the projection did not use it.
- **A responsive frontend** implementing the owner's Claude Design source: a collapsible tier board, a signed-gap draft rail, two sortable tables, three player-card variants by viewport, and a Data view that states the methodology once.
- **CSV export** — the full versioned artifact, and a filtered export of exactly the rows on screen in the order they are on screen.
- **An automated daily refresh** on GitHub Actions, deploying to GitHub Pages through a three-job graph in which nothing clears the live site before a new build validates.

Nine presets: STD, HALF and PPR × 10, 12 and 14 teams.

## Known V1 limitations

These are measured, published, and deliberately not repaired by moving a threshold.

- **Tier boundaries are statistically soft.** The stability gate failed on boundary agreement (0.239 against 0.500) and the tiers ship with the failure attached. Membership is reproducible; exact cut positions are not. The board draws a tier as a band and never as a line, for this reason.
- **Player simulations are independent, not team-correlated.** V1 cannot express that a quarterback's collapse takes his receivers with him. The largest structural simplification in the simulation, and unmeasured.
- **MyFantasyLeague is the only market price source**, and it exposes a PPR flag with no half-PPR filter — so a standard- or half-scoring reader is looking at a board priced mostly by PPR drafters. Said plainly on the Data view.
- **Arbitrage is a deterministic market-gap baseline, not a learned model.** No surplus and no probability is claimed, because the historical market data needed to train one is dense but not point-in-time. Retention started in Phase 5; a learned target needs several more seasons of it.
- **Injury and roster status is annotation, never a model feature.**
- **Residual Monte Carlo error is real.** At 10,000 draws the ranking criteria converge and the value criteria miss their tolerance by 19–29%. A build is deterministic for a fixed seed; it is not seed-invariant.

## Not in V1, and deliberately so

Multi-source market pricing including Fantasy Football Calculator, a learned arbitrage model, historical injury features, and correlated-player simulation are all recorded as post-V1 work with their preconditions written down. FantasyPros is registered `benchmark_only` and feeds nothing.
